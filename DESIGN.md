# Lantern Vale: design notes (paste this at the start of every session)

**Feeling:** quiet, cozy, curious. A gentle dawn. Nothing here wants to hurt you.
**No combat, no fail states, no timers.**

**Art rules**
- Smooth, rounded shapes only (lathe, sphere, smooth-shaded). No flat-shaded boxes.
- Soft vertex-colored terrain; warm light vs cool shadow; fog does the mood.
- Palette: dusk plum/violet to apricot dawn; moss/olive greens; cream, gold, rose accents.
- Cloth/hat colors: teal cloak, terracotta hat, mustard scarf. Fox: warm orange with cream.

**Sound rules:** soft pads, sparse pentatonic plucks, wind, stream, birds that arrive with the light.

**Core verbs:** walk, look, E to touch/wake/ring. Add very few new ones.

**Story so far (Chapter 1: The Vale Wakes):** wake at camp, read Grandmother Wren's note, take the lantern, light it at the fire, follow the fox, wake 3 lamps, ring the lighthouse bell.

**Chapter ideas:** the lighthouse door opens; a harbor village at first light; the fox starts trusting you; a night-sky chapter with constellations.

**Constraints:** Three.js r128 via cdnjs, single published artifact, <16 MB. Test with tests/playthrough.mjs before publishing.
